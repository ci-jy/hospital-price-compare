Negotiated rates from the hospitals' own price-transparency files, made comparable by payer and service. Rates are only compared within one comparison group: one billing code, setting, billing class, modifier set and pricing method.

The metro median is taken over every rate in the group. Each hospital row shows the median of its payers' rates, so a hospital with many payers on one contract can sit on either side of it.

How far each payer's rates sit from the metro median of their comparison group, over every dollar-priced group that has at least two hospitals. -20% means the payer's typical rate is a fifth below what the metro as a whole pays for the same code, setting and pricing method. Payers with fewer than 100 such rates are left out, and so are hospital cells with fewer than 20.
